Rules & Safety Center

A real enforcement layer for uploads, comments, community posts, Shorts and live features.

  1. No harassment, threats or hateful attacks.
    Targeted abuse, threats, slurs or attacks on protected groups are blocked.
  2. No sexual or exploitative content.
    Sexual content, sexual solicitation and exploitative material are blocked.
  3. No dangerous or illegal instructions.
    Content that meaningfully facilitates dangerous, violent or illegal activity is blocked.
  4. No spam, scams or deceptive engagement.
    Fraud, credential theft, fake giveaways, phishing and manipulative spam are blocked.
  5. Respect intellectual property.
    Copyright complaints can create strikes; only publish material you have permission to use.
  6. No impersonation or misleading identity.
    Do not present yourself as another creator, organization or official account.
  7. No malicious links or malware.
    Content intended to compromise devices or accounts is blocked.
Enforcement: ordinary violations escalate from warning → strike → temporary restriction → suspension → termination. High-severity violations can skip a warning. Repeated spam can trigger a temporary posting cooldown.
